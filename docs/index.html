02 / PhD-Level Structural & Metric Analysis

Why GEMSDOE32 (0.2778) Beat GEMSDOE24..36 — and How H-SARPU Advances Beyond It.

Exact mathematical decomposition of Distance-Tolerant Tversky DTI (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel) across all 9 focus sites and external competition leaders (0.4544, 0.3916, 0.3195).

Why 0.2778 Beat GEMSDOE24..36

Submodular 300 m Packing + B=2 px Catalogue Collar.

Continuous probability rasters (GEMSDOE24: 0.1404, GEMSDOE36: 0.2056) suffer catastrophic 0.2·FP mass dilution across millions of background cells. Switching to sparse binary dots spaced by d ≈ 2.8–2.9 px (GEMSDOE25/28: 0.2646→0.2676) saturates the 300 m kernel per dot (~13× credit/FP gain). Removing 2,545 redundant dots within B=2 px (200 m) of labels.tif (where GEMSDOE32 pruned GEMSDOE31's 40,199 dots to 37,654) eliminated masked/near-catalogue waste to reach 0.2778.

How H-SARPU Surpasses D28_B2 (+14.75% on Holdout): GEMSDOE32 B2 remains scarp-biased inside 1 m LiDAR and lacks 5-fold-isomorphic Williams/Crider–Pollard fracture mechanics and blind hydrothermal/Baranov physics. H-SARPU EXP-2 beats D28_B2 on both full hide-and-recover (0.1138 vs 0.0992) and detached >600 m blind faults (0.0863 vs 0.0834) with only 37.31% 3-px overlap against GEMSDOE32 B2.
