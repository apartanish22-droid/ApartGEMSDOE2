What may explain GEMSDOE32's reported 0.2778?

The archived geometry shows a reduced B2 dot set relative to related files, with removed dots concentrated near the mirrored known catalogue. Under distance-weighted Tversky scoring, removing redundant predictions might reduce false-positive mass when they add no new truth credit; pruning could also remove true positives. Without hidden labels and a receipt tied to exact bytes, this is a mechanism hypothesis, not proof of why 0.2778 occurred or evidence that a new file can beat it.
