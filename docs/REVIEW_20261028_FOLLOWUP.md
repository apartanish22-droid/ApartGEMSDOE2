## Split geometry: a genuine blocker, not a new score

The unchanged pinned `src/gems32/holdout.py` (`c476410a…`) selects connected components, erodes quadrant domains and uses a 15-pixel dilation around quadrants for training exclusion. The claimed 15-pixel buffer is not a separation between each withheld truth pixel and the visible catalogue. Replaying the split geometry against hash-verified owner-mirrored sample, labels and SGMC:

- **22,232** withheld-positive occurrences across eight draw/fold cells; repeated draws mean these are not necessarily distinct faults.
- **15,705** occurrences are within 15 pixels of visible catalogue cells; the nearest distance is **2 pixels in every cell**.
- **76** selected connected-component instances with scored truth are partially clipped; connected components are not necessarily geological fault segments.

[Machine-readable receipt and input hashes](../evidence/shared_split_geometry_20261028.json) · [reproduction script](../scripts/audit_shared_splits.py) · [canonical issue #60](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/issues/60).
