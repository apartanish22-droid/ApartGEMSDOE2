DO NOT DOWNLOAD OR UPLOAD THE H-SARPU TIFF/ZIP.

Files linked from the H-SARPU archive are research artifacts only. Do not copy their filename or note into DrivenData. A local format check or historic HOLDOUT-DTI diagnostic is not submission approval.

What must happen before any future submission

  1. Use a user-assigned, preregistered method lane and the canonical shared feature stack, evaluator, writer and complete registry. Resolve shared defects upstream; do not run a private fork.
  2. Obtain and hash-verify the organizer's current inputs and sample/template. Rebuild and audit the live registry contents after source updates; current source-head changes alone do not establish raster identity.
  3. Run whole-fault-segment holdout with the specified buffer; recompute every catalogue-derived feature from visible faults only and mask visible faults pixel-exactly. Run each-feature leakage canaries.
  4. Require paired, uncertainty-aware HOLDOUT-DTI to beat the frozen same-protocol baseline. Report evaluator version, withheld-positive count and 95% CI. Do not convert it to a leaderboard projection.
  5. Check the candidate surface against the full registry before placement and final dots after placement. Stop on rank-correlation above 0.90 or more than 70% within three pixels of any registry raster. Resolve the registered-lattice conflict with rule owners; do not game or silently exempt it.
  6. Validate the exact saved TIFF against the authenticated competition template: single band, CRS, shape, geotransform, finite in-footprint predictions in [0,1], correct exterior nodata, and no NaN inside footprint. Record SHA-256 and automated validator output.
  7. Only a separate selector may approve a named download and note (at most 140 characters) after all gates clear and the official current weekly allowance is checked. Keep the receipt bound to the uploaded file hash.

These steps are conditions for a future release; they are not evidence that this candidate passed them. At present the project must keep the upload action unavailable.
