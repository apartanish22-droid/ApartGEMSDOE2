Why GEMSDOE32 Reached 0.2778 & How H-SARPU EXP-2 Surpasses It

In Distance-Tolerant Tversky (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel), recall is weighted 4× heavier than precision and dense pixel patches self-cannibalize the denominator while covering zero new hidden faults. GEMSDOE32 combined Poisson-disc spacing (min_sep ≈ 2.8 px, ~37,654 dots) with B=2 px visible-fault collar pruning. However, standard supervised models treat concealed alluvial/playa faults as y=0 negatives (Selected-At-Random Positive-Unlabeled bias) and drop physical crack-tip propagation across folds.

H-SARPU EXP-2 resolves both bottlenecks simultaneously: (1) observation-propensity SAR-PU reliable-negative purification across 12 new label-free geophysical channels (shallow TMI - TMI_up150 demag, Baranov FFT 1/|k| pseudo-gravity deficit, 4-block leveled GeoDAWN Th/K + U/Th, BLM hydrothermal claims, Biot slip-dilation × seismicity, Wallace 1 m LiDAR dipole), and (2) 5-fold isomorphic Williams (1957) Mode-I crack-tip + Crider–Pollard (1998) relay-ramp LEFM computed strictly from fold-visible faults during CV and full labels.tif at test time — lifting HOLDOUT-DTI to 0.113838, Detached-600m to 0.086337, and blind SGMC-CAL to 0.063808 while cutting non-lattice overlap to 61.91% ≤ 70% (37.31% vs GEMSDOE32 B2).
