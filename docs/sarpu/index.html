Methods and source limits

The archived method combined selected-at-random positive-unlabeled learning, label-free geophysical channels, and elastic-fracture-mechanics-inspired features. Its named non-fault mimics include Lake Lahontan shoreline benches, saline playa clay, lithologic contacts, roads/rail, and survey-line artifacts. This is fault-propensity research, not direct confirmation of geothermal vents.
