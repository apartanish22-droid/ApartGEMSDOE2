Why the earlier “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” error cannot occur here

The training features encode nodata as −3.4028235e+38, including 3,061 px inside the footprint; any pipeline that propagates a feature band unchecked leaks that sentinel into the submission. The canonical writer used here clips to [0,1], writes 0.0 outside the footprint (nodata=None), then re-reads the saved bytes and asserts all 12,279,160 cells are finite and inside [0,1] before releasing the file. The independent validator repeats the check.

Gate status. Format: PASS. Scientific auto-promote under the preregistered gates: negative (paired-Δ 95% CI [-0.0015, +0.0152]; broad-registry overlap 74.5% vs one unscored raster). Spending the slot is therefore an explicit selector/owner decision, not something this page can authorise under the parallel-run protocol.
