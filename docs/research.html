Research: hypotheses, experiments, and what failed

Five ranked hypotheses were written before implementation in the preregistration (docs/research/ap2-preregistration-20261027.md in the GitHub repo). Lane executed: AP2-H1 CCCB test; final emitted field is EXP1 OOF CTRL because the chain arm lost — 4-quadrant OOF HistGB over the canonical 35-ch stack + 20 hash-pinned external channels, PU-aware LiDAR-gap negative weighting, chain corroboration, exact 300 m emitter, B=2. AP2-H2 (blind-basin reallocation) and AP2-H3 (dip migration) were deferred to stay in-lane; AP2-H4 (SGMC-difference) rejected (would invalidate the SGMC evaluator; measured live ≈0.05 when tried solo); AP2-H5 (raw seismicity) is data-blocked in this sandbox (named official source: USGS ComCat FDSN — egress here is GitHub/PyPI only).

Negative results (deliverables). (1) The chain gate hurt both metrics — along-strike continuity of a per-pixel probability ridge at 100 m does not separate faults from Lake Lahontan shorelines/basalt margins in this stack. (2) The first, literal implementation of the preregistered emitter (min_sep_px=2.8 on the raw field) saturated at 20,223 dots regardless of budget, and the first champion control was censorship-biased (0.0045). Both bugs, the fixes, and the retained flawed receipts are documented in docs/research/ap2-pass2-amendment-20261027.md and evidence/ap2_experiments_flawed_v1_minsep.json.

Evaluator verification. The vendored evaluator reproduces upstream GEMSDOE32's published control bit-exactly: D28 with visible-only B=2 → 0.09920232315251679 (22,232 withheld).

What would actually move the score (next sessions). (a) GPU full-footprint Tversky-loss segmentation on the 55-ch stack (impossible here: 2 vCPU / 3.8 GB, verified); (b) label-free physics channels that find different secondary ridges — the binding constraint is uniqueness vs the modern unscored registry, not another GBM on the same ridges; (c) an organiser receipt for one AP2-class file, without which holdout→live transfer above 0.2778 remains uncalibrated.
