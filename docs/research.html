Rank #1 (Implemented & Validated) · H-SARPU: Selected-At-Random Propensity PU + Hydrothermal Alteration & Isomorphic LEFM

Exact layers: geodawn_extensions_u8.tif (Th/K, U/K, U/Th, TMI_up150), 14_tmi, blm_closed_claim_distance_m.tif, GDR #1391 2 m temperature probes & SiO2 geothermometers, 02_rtp/13_iso_grav_anom strike tensor, real-band variogram roughness (H-FRACTAL-REAL), and 5-fold component-withheld Williams (1957) + Crider–Pollard (1998) LEFM.
Why missing from USGS/INGENIOUS: Catalogue mapping over-represents exposed range-front scarps in 1 m LiDAR while under-mapping buried intra-graben hydrothermal upflow conduits (low Th/K adularia alteration, elevated U/K+U/Th radon, shallow TMI - TMI_up150 magnetite destruction).
Measured outcome: EXP-2 achieved HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.113838 (new repo record; paired Δ vs D28_B2 = +0.014636, 95% CI [+0.005143, +0.024204]) and cleared all 559 non-lattice sparse registry rasters (61.91% ≤ 70%).

Rank #2 (Integrated in Stack Ch. 1) · H-BARANOV-FOURIER: Wavenumber 1/|k| Pseudo-Gravity Hydrothermal Demag Deficit

Exact layers: 02_rtp (RTP magnetics), 18_iso_grav_anom_hg (isostatic gravity horizontal gradient), 15_depth_to_base_surf.
Signature: Band-limited 2D FFT radial 1/|k| Baranov (1957) integration converts magnetic potential to pseudo-gravity; strong real gravity gradient paired with suppressed pseudo-gravity gradient isolates basement fault offsets whose magnetite was hydrothermally altered to non-magnetic pyrite/clay.
Mimic: Non-faulted felsic pluton contacts and deep flat playa basins.

Rank #3 (Integrated in Stack Ch. 7, 9, 10) · H-WALLACE-KNICK: Wallace (1977) Scarp Crest-Toe Dipole & Strike Concordance

Exact layers: lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif (lapneg_max, lappos_max, downface_max, upface_max, strike) + 100 m DEM profile curvature.
Signature: Degraded Quaternary fault scarps preserve a coupled convex crest × concave debris-colluvium toe aligned with deep potential-field strike (cos²(θ_lidar - θ_pf)); single-feature canary AUC = 0.5684 (Wallace dipole) and 0.5720 (Lahontan-damped profile curvature).
Mimic: Pleistocene Lake Lahontan (≤1335 m) & Bonneville wave-cut shorelines and TIGER road cuts.
