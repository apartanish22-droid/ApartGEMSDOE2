Research slate · untested

Four ideas. No
manufactured scores.

These are qualitative hypotheses, not assigned lanes or predictions. Their ranking is a planning judgment, and novelty beyond this checkout is not established.

These four hypotheses were not run in this review.

A separate parallel session ran a different, self-selected AP-1 idea and reported a negative result. Data access, lane assignment, registry completeness, the spent budget and the literal uniqueness rule block further testing here.

01 / Cover-aware positive–unlabeled learning

Layers: reduced-to-pole magnetics, magnetic/gravity gradients and detrended elevation. Seek a coincident subsurface contrast where topographic expression is weak; train fold-locally, treating unlabeled cells as a mixture rather than certain negatives. Mimics: lithologic contacts, volcanic boundaries and flight-line stripes. Difference: a PU replacement for the local supervised dual-cover learner. Cost: medium; inputs are owner mirrors, not authenticated, and the shared stack is not verified. Improvement: unknown; not run.

02 / Paired drainage offsets

Layers: USGS 3DEP and supplied detrended elevation/slope. Seek matching channels or fans displaced across a line. Mimics: stream capture and fan avulsion. Difference: paired displacement rather than single-ridge coherence. Cost: high; exact official tiles and coverage not fetched. Improvement: unknown; not run.

03 / Flight-line robustness

Layers: GeoDAWN magnetic flight lines, heights and gravity gradient. Seek edges stable in independent line-subset grids. Mimics: lithologic contacts and systematic leveling artifacts. Difference: line-withholding rather than grid smoothing. Cost: high; required bytes and coverage not verified. Improvement: unknown; not run.

04 / Hydrochemical compartment contrast

Layers: INGENIOUS well/spring chemistry and temperatures plus DEM. Test for across-boundary chemistry differences. Mimics: lithology, pumping and groundwater mixing. Difference: paired chemical contrast rather than proximity. Cost: high; actual bytes, coverage and comparability not verified. Improvement: unknown; not run.

What is needed before testing?

Verify a method assignment, restore the original licensed data and cached stack, freeze a complete registry and a same-evaluator best, pre-register the experiment, and resolve the literal lattice issue without silently exempting it. Whole-component buffered validation, one-feature leakage canaries, both uniqueness stages and a separate selector remain mandatory.
