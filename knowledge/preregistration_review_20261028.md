**Expected relative effects, not forecasts:** (1) PU learning might reduce the bias from treating all unmapped faults as true negatives, improving precision or recall at a matched dot budget; (2) paired channel/fan offsets might add far-field detections with direct geomorphic evidence; (3) line-subset stability might reduce acquisition-driven false positives; (4) cross-boundary chemistry might improve precision in well-sampled hydrothermal compartments. The sign and magnitude of every effect are unknown and any could lose to the matched control. A proposed improvement is not a score or a substitute for the positive paired holdout interval.

The physical interpretation is supported only in general terms by the [organizer's fault description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/968/), the [USGS GeoDAWN release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and), and [USGS work on magnetic flight-line noise](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2002/0098/intro.htm). Those sources do not validate the proposed models or predict their scores. The task is fault detection, not the direct discovery of a geothermal vent.
