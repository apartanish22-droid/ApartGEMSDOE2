## 3. Frozen Lane Paragraph (`H-UDSR`) & 3-Experiment Budget

> **Declared Lane (`H-UDSR`)**: Map unmapped geothermal-indicative surface fault traces across the Great Basin by projecting subsurface potential-field tilt-depth boundaries (`iso_grav_anom`, `rtp`) and magnetotelluric electrical conductance (`cond_surf`) **up-dip** along the local footwall-directed basement gradient vector ($-\nabla_h z_{\text{base}}/\|\nabla_h z_{\text{base}}\|$) onto their surface outcrop locus, corroborated by geodetic transtensional dilation-shear potency, contractor-block-leveled GeoDAWN radiometric alteration/radon crests, and DOE GDR #1391 silica-geothermometer / 2 m thermal probe / paleo-sinter upflow plumes, fitted via a leak-free Dual-Cover OOF classifier (`M_Full` inside 75.37% 1 m LiDAR + quantile-matched LiDAR-free `M_Gap` inside the 24.63% LiDAR gap) and emitted at `37,654` binary `{0,1}` dots via axial crest sharpening and `min_sep_px = 2.9` submodular `gems32.emitter.emit` outside a `B = 2.0 px` ($200\text{ m}$) visible-catalogue flank mask.

### Named Non-Fault Mimic Processes & Physical De-Confounding
1. **Pleistocene Lake Lahontan wave-cut shoreline benches**: Sharp horizontal DEM steps at constant elevation contours lacking subsurface gravity/magnetic/MT roots -> suppressed by contour-parallelism penalty when potential-field tilt/updip support is absent.
2. **Closed-basin evaporative playas (uraniferous brines & conductive clays)**: High `cond_surf` and high raw `eU` in flat deep basins (`depth_to_base_surf > 300 m` and `det_elev_slope < 1.5 deg`) -> suppressed by flat-basin tectonic gate and per-block `eU` on `eTh` regression.
3. **Highway/railroad embankments & GeoDAWN survey block seams**: Linear DEM/radiometric artifacts -> removed via `tiger_road_distance_m.tif` distance damping (`< 150 m`) and per-contractor-block median/IQR standardization (`acquisition_block_id_100m.tif`).

### Three-Experiment Plan (Hard Cap = 3 Experiments)
1. **`EXP-1` (`H-UDSR-PurePhysics`)**: 100% label-free `H-UDSR` Updip Physical Crest Surface $S_{\text{phys}}$ + `min_sep_px = 2.9` submodular `emitter.emit` (`37,654` dots, `B = 2.0 px` visible-only mask). Establishes pure physical baseline without supervised ML.
2. **`EXP-2` (`H-UDSR-DualCover-OOF`)**: 53-channel label-free (`35` template + `18` `H-UDSR`) + 2 fold-visible tip/relay continuation channels Dual-Cover OOF HistGradientBoosting (`M_Full` + LiDAR-free `M_Gap`) fused with `S_phys`, 4-azimuth crest NMS, and `min_sep_px = 2.9` submodular `emitter.emit` (`37,654` dots, `B = 2.0 px`).
