## 3. Pre-Registered 3-Experiment Factorial (Zero Post-Hoc Tuning)

All three experiments share the identical 8-cell (`4 quadrants × draws 20/21`, `22,232` withheld positives) `GEMSDOE32-HIDE-RECOVER-v3.0` holdout split, identical 1.5 km spatial collar (`COLLAR_PX = 15`), identical $B = 2.0\text{ px}$ visible-only mask, and identical non-starving axial-crest candidate multiplier (`support_mult = 6.8`):

1. **`EXP-1` (`SPMD-Sup61-Ctrl`) — *61-Channel Supervised Content Control ($\gamma = 0.0$, unpurified negatives)*:**
   - Standard Dual-Cover training (`d_vis > 4 px` treated as negative, uniform weights, no spy purification) on the expanded **61-channel** `SPMD` stack, fused with fold-visible tip/relay continuation (`prior_mult = 1 + 2.4 * relay + 1.8 * tip`), single-stage exact submodular emission at `budget = 34,500`, `min_sep_px = 3.05`.
   - *Purpose*: Isolates the holdout lift of the 8 new shallow-bandpass / epithermal / dipolar-LiDAR channels + non-starving `support_mult = 6.8` emission prior to `SAR-PU`.
2. **`EXP-2` (`SPMD-SARPU-Unified`) — *Full `SAR-PU` Spy-Purified + Inverse-Propensity ($\gamma = 0.38$) Single-Stage Emission*:**
   - Subsurface spy teacher purges putative concealed faults above the 15th spy percentile from `d_vis > 4 px` unlabeled negatives; Dual-Cover `M_Full` (61 ch) and `M_Gap` (55 ch) train with positive inverse-observability weights $\hat{e}(x_{\text{obs}})^{-0.38}$ and inference propensity tempering $\hat{e}(x_{\text{obs}})^{-0.22}$, fused with fold-visible continuation (`prior_mult = 1 + 2.2 * relay + 1.6 * tip`), single-stage exact submodular emission at `budget = 34,500`, `min_sep_px = 3.05`.
   - *Purpose*: Tests whether `SAR-PU` reliable-negative purification + propensity de-confounding lifts holdout recovery above both `EXP-1` and the archived repo best (`0.113696`).
3. **`EXP-3` (`SPMD-SARPU-TwoRegime`) — *Full `SAR-PU` ($\gamma = 0.45$) + Balanced Corridor/Subsurface-Far-Field Submodular Allocation*:**
   - Same `SAR-PU` spy-purified Dual-Cover models (`\gamma_train = 0.38`), paired with stronger far-field exposure de-confounding (`\hat{e}(x_{\text{obs}})^{-0.45}` blended with pure subsurface/matched-depth/epithermal conduit score `S_spmd_sub` in `d > 18 px`) and two-stage residual submodular emission (`budget = 33,600`, `min_sep_px = 3.12`: **64% (`21,504` dots)** in the structural corridor `2 < d <= 18 px` + **36% (`12,096` dots)** in the propensity-deconfounded blind far-field `d > 18 px`).
   - *Purpose*:Simultaneously preserves high near/stepover recall (`> 21,000` corridor dots) to beat both `D28_B2` (`0.099202`) and archived `H-UDSR EXP-2` (`0.113696`) while shifting far-field predictions off bare-rock topographic ridges (`8GEMSDOE_Hedge-v2`, `wbggems3-hce1`, `gems10-h25`) onto concealed intrabasin/epithermal conduits so **every non-lattice sparse registry raster stays strictly `<= 70%` within 3 px**.
