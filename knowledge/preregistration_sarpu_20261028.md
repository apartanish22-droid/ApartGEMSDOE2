### 3.2 Selected-At-Random Propensity-Decontaminated PU (`SAR-PU`) Dual-Cover Architecture
Within each spatial training domain (`foot & ~collars[fold]`):
1. **Observation Propensity Model $\hat{e}(x)$:** Fit a monotonic/regularized shallow propensity model on exposure covariates $X_{\text{obs}} = (\text{det\_elev\_slope}, \text{relief5}, \text{tophat2}, \text{depth\_to\_base\_surf}, \text{lidar\_valid})$ predicting catalogued vs background piedmont/range pixels; compute clipped inverse propensity weights on labeled positives $w_+(x) = \text{clip}(\bar{e}_+ / \hat{e}(x), 0.75, 2.85)$ so low-relief / buried positives receive higher weight than over-sampled steep LiDAR scarps.
2. **Two-Stage Reliable-Negative PU Decontamination:**
   - Stage 1 pilot pure-physical model scores sampled unlabeled candidates ($d_{\text{vis}} > 4\text{ px}$).
   - Unlabeled pixels scoring in the top `4%` of pilot fault probability OR top `3%` of `sarpu_blind_conduit_index` (likely unmapped blind/bedrock faults contaminating $U$!) are excised from hard $y=0$ negatives, and remaining reliable negatives are weighted by $1 - 0.5\hat{p}_{\text{pilot}}(x)$.
3. **Dual-Cover Calibrated Prediction:** Separate `M_Full` (all `65` pure-physical channels inside `lidar_valid`) and `M_Gap` (`60` strictly `1 m`-LiDAR-free channels across the `24.66%` LiDAR gap, quantile-aligned to `M_Full` on held-in `lidar_valid` calibration pixels) prevent step-edge seam artifacts across missing 3DEP tiles. A parallel **Coupled LEFM + SAR-PU** pair (`M_Coupled_Full` on `67` ch, `M_Coupled_Gap` on `62` ch) additionally ingests the 5-group component-jackknifed Williams/Crider–Pollard channels.
