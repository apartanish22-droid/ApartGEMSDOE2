## New candidate hypotheses — unranked proposals for the next lane (not yet run)

| Rank | Hypothesis / layers and signature | Why it might find a missed fault | Non-fault mimic | Difference / cost / data gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **H-DIL-GRAV: geodetic dilatation × gravity low.** Layers: training_features band 8 `geod_dilaterate` (volumetric strain rate from GPS/InSAR) and band 13 `iso_grav_anom` (isostatic gravity anomaly). Signature: positive dilatation rate coincident with a negative gravity anomaly, both normalized to [0,1] and multiplied. | Concealed normal faults in the extensional Great Basin localize dilation; hydrothermal alteration/fracturing lowers bulk density → a gravity low co-located with the concealed fault zone that controls geothermal upflow but need not break the surface as a mapped scarp. | Broad sedimentary basins produce gravity lows without faults; regional tectonic extension produces dilation without a discrete fault. | Uses two already-available bands (no external download, zero cost). Distinct from H1 radiometric×curvature (no radiometric bands), from scarp/openness/dotted-ridge families (no surface expression required), and from H2 fractal texture (real measured bands, not pseudo-features). **Viable now.** |
| 2 | **H-MAG-EDGE×SLOPE: magnetic horizontal gradient × detrended elevation slope.** Layers: band 3 `tmi_hg` and band 19 `det_elev_slope`. Signature: strong magnetic edge coincident with steep topography. | A fault displacing magnetic basement produces a gradient edge; steep residual topography marks a scarp not in the catalogue (e.g. buried by alluvium but steep enough in the detrended DEM). | Lithologic contacts, volcanic boundaries, degradation/erosion scarps. | Uses real bands; distinct from H1 (no gravity) and from scarp families (multiplies by magnetic edge, so flat alluvium-covered scarps are suppressed unless magnetic). **Viable now.** |
| 3 | **H-DEPTH×COND: depth to basement × conductivity surface.** Layers: band 15 `depth_to_base_surf` and band 17 `cond_surf`. Signature: conductive signature under thin sediment cover. | A fault-related conductive fracture zone beneath thin cover can host geothermal upflow without any surface expression in the catalogue. | Clay-rich or saline sedimentary intervals, inversion artifacts. | Real bands; distinct from all runoff/dilation/magnetic work. **Viable now.** |
| 4 | **H-2NDINV×DILATE: geodetic second invariant × dilatation rate.** Layers: bands 4 and 8. Signature: high strain-rate magnitude with volumetric expansion. | Strain partitioning marks active fault zones; dilatation isolates extensional (normal-fault) kinematics relevant to geothermal permeability. | Broad flexural warping, non-tectonic ground deformation. | Real bands; distinct from H-DIL-GRAV (adds strain magnitude; no gravity). **Viable now.** |
| 5 | **H-SHEAR: geodetic shear rate alone.** Layer: band 7 `geod_shearrate`. Signature: localized angular deformation. | Shear-rate localization can mark strike-slip or oblique faults with no scarp and no gravity expression. | Landslides, creeping slopes, non-tectonic deformation. | Single band, zero cost; weakest expected signal because no co-located geothermal qualifier. **Viable now.** |

Ranking by expected DTI improvement per implementation cost: **1 H-DIL-GRAV** (geothermal-specific mechanism: dilatation opens permeability; gravity low marks the low-density altered fault core; both measured, not modelled) > 2 MAG-EDGE×SLOPE > 3 DEPTH×COND > 4 2NDINV×DILATE > 5 SHEAR.

Each candidate's named non-fault mimic must be tested on the holdout; any single feature whose rank AUC against held-out truth exceeds 0.90 is treated as leakage (Protocol Rule 4) and rejected.

## Mandatory validation after a new budget and an actual lane are assigned

Pre-register the mechanism and actual unique data products; confirm no catalogue leakage; build all label-dependent features from fold-visible faults only; withhold whole connected fault segments with a spatial buffer; mask visible catalogue pixels exactly; use the shared evaluator and writer; run standalone feature leakage canaries; compare pooled HOLDOUT-DTI and a paired spatial-block 95% CI with a frozen same-protocol best; check the complete registry before placement and on final dots; validate saved bytes against the authenticated original template; and use a separate automated selector. The public score of an unseen expert-mapped target cannot be inferred from a holdout ratio alone.

The [official about page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/968/) describes faults as potential fluid conduits, not proof of active geothermal vents. [USGS cautions](https://pubs.usgs.gov/mf-maps/mf1136/mf1136/limit.htm) that non-fault processes can resemble scarps; [GeoDAWN](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) documents variable flight height and survey processing. These support cautions, not the claimed performance of the proposals.
